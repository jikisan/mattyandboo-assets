Questions

Good to know

What do I get?

A digital, editable template. No physical item is shipped. You’ll find the template link in your Etsy purchase after checkout.

How do I edit it?

Open the template link in Canva, in your browser or the Canva app, and type in your own details. Instructions come with every purchase.

Can I print it?

Yes. Download it from Canva and print at home or at a local print shop.

Can I send it as an e-invite?

Yes. Download it as an image and send it by text, WhatsApp, email or any chat app.

Something not working?

Send us a message through Etsy and we’ll help. Refunds and exchanges follow our shop policies on Etsy.
