Privacy policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

This policy explains what information Matty and Boo (“we”, “us”) handles on this website and in the Matty and Boo Scheduler, the tool we use to publish posts to our own social media accounts. Contact: mattyandbooshop@gmail.com.

This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics, advertising trackers or forms, and it does not ask for any personal information. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log basic technical data such as IP addresses to keep the service secure. See GitHub’s privacy statement for details.

Purchases

Our products are sold on Etsy. When you buy from us, Etsy processes your order and payment under Etsy’s own privacy policy. We receive only the order details Etsy shares with sellers, and we use them only to deliver your order and answer your questions.

Matty and Boo Scheduler

The Matty and Boo Scheduler is an internal tool used only by Matty and Boo. It connects to our own Instagram, Pinterest, TikTok and Facebook accounts through each platform’s official login, so we can schedule and publish our own posts and read their performance statistics.

  • What it accesses: our own account profile, the posts we publish, and statistics for those posts.
  • What it stores: access tokens for our own accounts and the content of our scheduled posts, kept on our own device.
  • What it does not do: it does not collect data about other users, does not sell or share data, and does not use data for advertising.

Deleting data

To have any data about you removed, or to ask what we hold, email mattyandbooshop@gmail.com. We will reply within 30 days. Disconnecting the scheduler from a platform deletes its access tokens for that account.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
